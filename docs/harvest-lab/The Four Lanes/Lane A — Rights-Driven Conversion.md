# Lane A — Rights-Driven Conversion (the mission)

> [!info] In a line
> The original mission: convert an author's accepted manuscript into JATS under Manchester's Rights Retention Policy — because otherwise the work is locked behind a paywall and *nobody else will ever make it open*.

This is the workstation you already have, unchanged. It lives in its own repository
(`aam-to-jatsxml`), keeps its own *vault*, and is included in the harvest
workstation as the `lane_a/` submodule — which is also where the shared gates
that judge every package come from.

## Why it's special

Lane A is not about CC-BY at all. It processes **author accepted manuscripts** — the author's final
text before the publisher's typesetting — deposited under the University's Rights Retention Policy.
The published version of these papers is often paywalled, so there is *no* structured open version to
harvest. If Manchester doesn't convert the manuscript, the research stays behind a paywall.

That makes Lane A **irreplaceable and rights-driven**. It's the only lane whose value doesn't depend
on someone else having already done the work — because, by definition, they haven't.

## Why the harvest lanes don't threaten it

Everything in this lab is about the *published* version of record and its CC-BY licence. Lane A is
about the *manuscript* and a different legal basis entirely. The two never compete:

- A paper that is paywalled-but-has-a-Manchester-AAM → **Lane A**.
- A paper that is CC-BY-published → one of the harvest/convert lanes (B, C, C′).

If anything, the harvest lanes *protect* Lane A: by taking thousands of easy CC-BY papers off the
workstation's plate, they free its scarce attended time for the manuscripts that genuinely need
hand-conversion.

## The standard it sets

Lane A also sets the quality bar the harvest lanes must clear. Its gates — structural validation,
house-style lint, content-fidelity check, independent AI read-back — are re-run on *every* package
regardless of where it came from. An ingested paper (Lane C) is treated as untrusted until it passes
the same gates a hand-converted one does. See *The Benchmark — Proof It Works*.
